        How to play

        Esc

        Onigokko is tag. One player is the oni - they are "it". Touch someone and it passes to them. Every little picture below is the real game, playing itself.

        1Tag

        The oni runs a touch faster, and whoever was just tagged cannot tag straight back. In Hot Potato, the oni at the buzzer loses. In Least Time, the least time spent as the oni wins.

        2Run and jump

        Hold jump longer to go higher, and press it again in the air for a second jump. Down drops you through thin ledges, and makes you fall faster.

        3Slopes

        Walk up and down them. They work like thin ledges: jump up through them from below, hold down to drop through or to pass underneath.

        4Trampolines and launchers

        A trampoline throws you straight up - in a real arena, far higher than in this little picture. A launcher, marked with arrows, flings you up and across. Steer in the air.

        5Updrafts

        A column of rising air. Step in, let go, and ride it up; steer out at any height. You get your second jump back inside.

        6Portals

        Step in and you fly to the matching portal as a spark, in an arc over the arena. Nobody can tag you on the way. Portals with an arrow mark let you drop out mid-flight: press jump.

        7Thorns

        They do not tag you, but they sting: a short stun and a bounce off them - just enough for the oni to catch up.

        8Lucky boxes

        Touch a box with empty hands to draw one of eight items, then set it off when it suits you with E with the item button. Nothing works in the last 10 seconds of a round.

          9Team Bomb

          Sun against Moon. Flags: stand in a flag's ring for seconds and it turns your colour; it then pays your team until the other side stands there long enough to turn it. Bombs: one for every two players. Touch an enemy whose hands are free to stick yours on them. Every bomb goes off when the clock stops - and scores for the team that is not holding it.

          Controls

          • ←→ or AD Run
          • ↑ W or Space Jump - again in the air for a second jump
          • ↓ or S Drop through thin ledges and slopes, fall faster
          • E X or Shift Use the item in your hands
          • Esc Pause menu
          • Left thumb strip: run. Slide across it to turn without lifting your thumb.
          • Big button: jump - press again in the air for a second jump.
          • Arrow-down button: drop through thin ledges and slopes, fall faster.
          • Item button: lights up when you hold an item; press it to set the item off.
          • Menu button (top right): pause, sound, leave.

          On a phone or tablet you get a thumb strip for running and buttons for jump, down and the item.

          Keyboard shortcuts

          • ↑↓ Move through any menu
          • ←→ Change the choice you are on (rules, arena, style)
          • Enter Choose - in a room it starts the match
          • Esc Back - during a match it opens the pause menu
          • 1 2 3 Title screen: play, practice, your profile
          • C P 0-9 Play: host a game, public games, type a private code
          • G R N Match setup: one match or a tournament, which game, how many rounds
          • A S T Match setup: next arena, next style (with Shift: previous); in a tournament, T picks which round they are for
          • L P B Match setup: round length, lucky boxes, bots
          • C V In a room: copy the invite link, make the room public or private
          • X Z Team battle, in a room: change sides; the host shuffles the teams
          • H Menus: how to play
          • M F Anywhere: sound on or off, full screen

          Tournament

          • 3, 5 or 7 rounds. Each round, the less time you spent as the oni, the more points you get.
          • Everyone who is not the oni at the buzzer gets a bonus point.
          • The leader starts the next round as the oni. Every second round has a twist. The last round counts double.
          • With the arena or the style on Random, the draw before each round picks a new one - a tournament visits every arena before it repeats one.
          • Team Bomb tournament: the teams stay together and their points add up over the rounds.
