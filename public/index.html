      How to play

      Esc

      One player is it. Touch someone to pass it on. In Hot Potato, whoever is it when the clock hits zero loses. In Least Time, the player who spent the least time as it wins.

      Controls

      Left thumb strip: run (slide to change direction). Big button: jump - press again in the air for a second jump. Small button: drop through thin ledges and slopes, fall faster.

      • ←→ or AD Run
      • ↑ W or Space Jump - press again in the air for a second jump
      • ↓ or S Drop through thin ledges and slopes, fall faster

      Around the arena

      • Trampolines launch you high.
      • Updrafts (columns of rising air) carry you up - let go and ride.
      • Portals turn you into a spark and fly you to the matching portal. Nobody can tag you on the way.
      • Slopes can be walked up and down, and jumped through from below.

      Power-ups

      • Swift Run faster for 6 s
      • Spring Jump higher for 7 s
      • Ward Blocks one tag
      • Frost Freezes the other side for a moment

      Keyboard shortcuts

      • ↑↓ Move through any menu
      • ←→ Change the choice you are on (rules, arena, style)
      • Enter Choose - in a room it starts the match
      • Esc Back - during a match it opens the pause menu
      • 123 Title screen: create, join, practice
      • A S Match setup: next arena, next style (with Shift: previous)
      • GRLPB Match setup: game, rules or rounds, length, power-ups, bots
      • C In a room: copy the invite link
      • M F Anywhere: sound on or off, full screen

      Tournament

      • Several rounds. Each round, the less time you spent as it, the more points you get.
      • Everyone who is not it at the buzzer gets a bonus point.
      • The leader starts the next round as it. Every second round has a twist. The last round counts double.
